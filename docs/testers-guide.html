Get LIFT

LIFT builds your training plan from a few questions, runs your workout at the gym, and adds weight when you're ready. It's free while it's in testing. Setup takes about two minutes on your iPhone.

  1. Install TestFlight

    TestFlight is Apple's free app for trying apps before they're on the App Store.

    Get TestFlight on the App Store

  2. Open the invite on your iPhone

    Tap the link on your phone, then Accept and Install. LIFT shows up on your home screen like any other app.

    Join the LIFT beta

    testflight.apple.com/join/DNFM3WyJ

  3. Answer the setup questions

    About a minute: pounds or kilos, your goal, experience, the days you train, how long a session is, your gym (pick all the places you train), anything that hurts, and what you lift now. LIFT builds your plan from that.

  4. Start your first workout

    Tap Start on today's workout. Type the weight once and it fills every set. Tick a set when it's done and the rest timer starts.

At the gym

Rest timer on your lock screen

The countdown shows in the Dynamic Island and on the lock screen, and an alert sounds when rest is over. Allow notifications the first time it asks.

Up next

The rest bar tells you the next set, or the next exercise and its weight, so you can walk over and set up.

Watch

Not sure how an exercise goes? Tap Watch for a form video.

Swap

Machine taken? Tap Swap and pick an alternative that works the same muscles.

It gets heavier on its own

Hit every rep and the weight goes up next time. Miss and it holds. Stall three times and it backs off so you can build up again.

Redo your plan

Settings → Plan & schedule → Redo onboarding. Your history stays.

Found a bug or have an idea?

This is an early build, so tell me everything. In LIFT, take a screenshot or shake your phone. A report form opens with that screen attached; write what happened and tap Send. You can also use Settings → Report a bug.

Good to know

  • Updates: TestFlight tells you when there's a new version. Open TestFlight and tap Update. Each build works for 90 days, so keep it updated.
  • Your data: everything stays on your phone for now. Settings → Data & backup exports a copy.
  • If the link says it's not accepting testers: the build is still with Apple for a quick review. Try again later that day.
  • You need an iPhone on a recent iOS. The lock-screen timer needs iOS 16.2 or later.
